## ✨ New Features

- 🔊 Added an on-the-fly **pronunciation button** to the overlay — tap to hear the card read aloud via text-to-speech
- 🎚️ Added a **daily new-card limit** with optional **front-loading** — introduce new cards earlier in the day and stop once you hit your cap, configurable in Settings
- 📥 Added **Pleco XML import** for flashcards exported from Pleco

## 🎨 Visual

- 🈷️ Numbered pinyin from imports (e.g. "lian4jie1") now displays with tone marks ("liànjiē")

## 🐛 Fixes

- 🐛 Fixed a card that could get stuck repeating forever and never record its reviews, caused by a scheduling edge case after a lapse
- 🐛 Fixed ratings occasionally not being saved when the overlay closed immediately after you tapped
